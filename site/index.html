Selected studies

Every strategy is proven, or killed, on history before it gets near real money.

Repairing a Fair Value Gap strategy

Why did a textbook FVG setup fail, and what does it take to fix it?

  • Timeframe was the biggest lever. On daily bars, overnight gaps blew straight through stops. Moving to 1-hour bars fixed the backwards risk asymmetry.
  • Better entries. A limit order at the gap top instead of chasing the close cut risk to the gap size and flipped reward-to-risk positive.
  • Quality filters. Volume above 1.2× average and a stacked trend (EMA50 above EMA200) removed most low-conviction setups.
  • Out-of-sample check. The same settings, with no re-tuning, were tested across five large-cap names over 11 years of hourly data in TradingView. They held up on three of the five, so the work continues before any capital goes in.

Finding a signal that could never fire

A volume-weighted RSI rule produced zero trades. Why?

  • The rule required RSI below 30 and price above the 50-day EMA at the same time, which never happened once in five years of NVDA data.
  • Redefined it as a pullback in an uptrend (RSI 35–50, above EMA50, volume confirmation). It now trades, and is being forward-tested for a real sample.
  • Along the way, found and fixed a frozen RSI bug: the indicator was seeded from the oldest bars, so it never reflected recent price.

Machine learning without fooling myself

Can a random forest predict direction, honestly measured?

  • Built a consensus-plus-confidence forest and validated it walk-forward only: train on the past, test on the next unseen window, repeat.
  • Only one name cleared the edge threshold, so the model is in forward-test, not live trading.

Dealer gamma as a market regime

Does negative dealer gamma below the flip level predict faster downside?

  • Free options history doesn't exist, so instead of a fake backtest STAR logs real GEX daily for SPY, QQQ and IWM and tests the edge as the sample grows.
Principles

How I build

Real data or an honest error

No seeded or placeholder numbers, ever. If a feed is down, the dashboard says so.

No look-ahead

Backtests fill on the next bar, include slippage and costs, and size by risk.

Decide the verdict first

Pass/fail rules are written before the test runs, so nobody can move the goalposts afterwards.

Safety is hardwired

Paper-only gates, daily loss limits and secrets in the macOS Keychain, not in files.
